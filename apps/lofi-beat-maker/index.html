New to this? Your first beat in 4 steps
  1. Pick a vibe below. It loads a ready-made beat, so press Play and you'll hear something right away.
  2. Tap squares in the beat grid to add or remove hits. Each row is one sound, and the beat loops left to right. A good rule: kick on 1, snare on 5 and 13, hats on every other square.
  3. Change the key or the chords. Tap a bar to try a different chord. They all fit together, so nothing will sound wrong.
  4. Play the jam pads over the top. Every pad is in key. Then turn up the vinyl crackle and rain, and vibe.
